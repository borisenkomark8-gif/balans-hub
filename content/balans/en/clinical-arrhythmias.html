01 · Baseline

Baseline data before prescribing load

ParameterWhat to establish
DiagnosisAF/atrial flutter, supraventricular tachycardia, ventricular ectopy/tachycardia, bradyarrhythmia, channelopathy, unexplained palpitations
SymptomsPalpitations, pain, breathlessness, dizziness, presyncope/syncope; relation to exertion
Rhythm documentation12-lead ECG, Holter/monitor; watch data for screening only, not final diagnosis
Structural heartEchocardiography; cardiac MRI and ischemia assessment when indicated
Exercise testingECG test or CPET to reproduce symptoms, assess ventricular response, and arrhythmia threshold
DevicePacemaker/ICD type, detection/therapy zones, recent shocks, pacing dependence, post-implant restrictions
Treatmentβ-blockers/other antiarrhythmics, anticoagulation, ablation, QT-affecting drugs
Reversible factorsK, Mg, kidney function, anemia, thyroid function, infection, alcohol, stimulants, hypoglycemia, sleep apnea

03 · Prescription

General framework and intensity control

General framework

ParameterPrescription
Aerobic volume150–300 min moderate or 75–150 min vigorous per week if the specific arrhythmia and structural disease do not require restriction
Frequency3–5 days/week
Strength work2–3 days/week; 1–3 sets of 8–15 reps; no breath holding
ProgressionDuration first, then intensity; avoid sudden jumps in training volume

Intensity control

  • With sinus rhythm and adequate chronotropic response — heart rate from exercise test/CPET.
  • With AF, frequent ectopy, pacing, β-blockade, or chronotropic insufficiency — prioritize external power/speed, Borg 11–14/20 and symptoms; do not use pulse alone.
  • When arrhythmia is exertion-induced, set upper limit below documented threshold.
  • With ICD, agree maximum training heart rate with electrophysiology and leave margin to detection/therapy zone; margin depends on device and protocol, often 10–20 bpm.

04 · Special situations

Practical limits

SituationPractical limit
AFUse AF-CARE: treat comorbid factors, stroke prevention, symptom control, regular reassessment
AnticoagulationAvoid contact and high-injury sports; consider falls and bleeding risk
Pacemaker/ICD after implantShoulder and device-site load limits per implant team only; then gradual return of range and strength
Channelopathies/arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathyDo not apply generic advice automatically; sport volume set by specialist for diagnosis and genotype/phenotype
ElectrolytesCheck K/Mg and kidney function when clinically indicated; no routine supplements without deficiency/indication

AlcoholNo more than 3 standard drinks or 30 g alcohol per week; stricter limit/avoidance if episodes link to alcohol
Sleep apnea, hypertension, diabetes, heart failureActive detection and control as part of AF-CARE

06 · Safety

Stopping exercise

Stop immediately for syncope/presyncope, chest pain, marked hypotension, sustained fast rhythm with symptoms, new complex ventricular arrhythmia, ICD therapy, sudden unusual breathlessness, or neurological symptoms.

Document the episode. Time, activity, symptoms, device heart rate/ECG, blood pressure, medicines taken.
