What we use

BMI

Weight divided by height squared — a rough guide that does not separate muscle and fat mass.

A simple additional marker of body shape and metabolic risk.

Mifflin–St Jeor

Starting estimate of resting metabolic rate and daily energy needs when body composition is unknown.
